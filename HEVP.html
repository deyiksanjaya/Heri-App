02 · Overview

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how HEVP handles data when you use the app. HEVP is a creative audio performance and EVP-style recording studio intended for entertainment and creative expression.

HEVP does not claim to detect, prove, or communicate with paranormal entities. Any EVP-style experience is a creative feature, not a scientific or factual claim.

HEVP is developed by Heri Sanjaya I Gede. For questions, contact ighsforyou@gmail.com.

03 · Local by design

Information Stored on the Device

HEVP may store the following locally in the app’s storage on your device:

  • Audio recordings you create
  • Pre-recorded audio you select or create
  • Text templates and session configurations
  • Automatic sequences and app preferences
  • Control positions and display settings
  • Recording metadata such as name, duration, and creation time
  • An ElevenLabs API key entered by you

The ElevenLabs API key is stored securely using the iOS Keychain. The developer has no remote access to data that exists only on your device.

04 · Permission

Microphone Access

HEVP requests microphone access to create session recordings, create pre-recorded audio inside the app, and provide its core recording functionality. The microphone is used only after you grant permission.

You can review or revoke microphone permission at any time in iOS Settings. If you revoke access, recording features that require the microphone may no longer work.

05 · Apple system tools

Photos and File Selection

HEVP may use Apple’s system photo picker or file importer when you choose an image or audio file. Using a system picker does not give HEVP broad access to your entire photo library or file system. The app receives only the items you explicitly select.

06 · Optional third-party service

Optional ElevenLabs AI Voice

AI Voice is optional. It requires your own ElevenLabs API key and is used only when you choose to generate audio through that feature.

When you use AI Voice, HEVP may send directly to ElevenLabs:

  • Your ElevenLabs API key
  • The selected Voice ID
  • The text template or text you ask to convert to speech
  • Technical parameters needed to generate the audio

HEVP does not send your microphone recordings to ElevenLabs. Communication uses a direct HTTPS connection to ElevenLabs. ElevenLabs’ handling of data is governed by its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Please review those documents for current details, including retention and processing practices.

Any ElevenLabs fees or subscription are separate from your HEVP purchase and are part of your relationship with ElevenLabs.

07 · Transparency

Data Collection by the Developer

In its current version, HEVP:

  • Does not have user accounts or require a user profile
  • Does not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs
  • Does not perform cross-app tracking
  • Does not sell personal data
  • Does not operate a developer server for uploading user recordings

Text submitted through AI Voice is processed by the third-party provider to deliver the function you requested.

08 · Your choice

Sharing and Exporting Recordings

You can share recordings using the iOS share sheet. Once you select an app or service, the shared data is handled according to that destination’s privacy policy. The HEVP developer does not control how a destination service stores or uses a file you choose to share.

09 · Your device, your data

Data Retention and Deletion

Local recordings, audio, and settings remain on your device until you delete them in the app, delete the app, or erase or restore the device. Deleting the app generally removes data from its app container, but anything already exported, shared, or backed up may remain elsewhere.

To remove a recording, use the delete control provided inside HEVP wherever available. If the app provides a setting to manage the ElevenLabs API key, remove it from the app’s Settings. Do not rely on app deletion alone for copies you have exported or shared.

10 · Protection

Security

HEVP relies on the iOS application sandbox, stores the ElevenLabs API key in the iOS Keychain, and uses HTTPS for communication with ElevenLabs. HEVP does not include a built-in API key or production credential in the app.

No digital system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure. Keep your device, iOS version, and any third-party accounts up to date.

11 · Families

Children’s Privacy

HEVP is not designed to collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to the developer, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

12 · Global availability

International Users

HEVP is available through the Apple App Store and may be used in different countries. Information sent to ElevenLabs may be processed in countries where ElevenLabs or its infrastructure providers operate.

13 · Keeping this current

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to HEVP features, applicable law, or service providers. Material changes may be communicated through this website, the App Store listing, or an app update.
